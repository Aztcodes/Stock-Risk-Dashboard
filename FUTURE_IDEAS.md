## 1. Portfolio Analysis

Analyse a user's portfolio relative to their stated risk tolerance.

- User inputs their holdings (ticker + position size)
- Aztic runs scores across the portfolio and computes a weighted aggregate Aztic score
- Flags positions that are misaligned with the user's risk tolerance (e.g. high-risk stocks in a conservative portfolio)
- Could incorporate correlation / concentration risk (e.g. 60% in one sector)
- Output: portfolio-level risk dashboard + per-holding flags + rebalancing suggestions

---

## 2. Head-to-Head Stock Comparison

Side-by-side comparison of two tickers to help users decide between them.

- User enters two tickers (e.g. NVDA vs AMD)
- Generates both reports in parallel and renders them in a split view
- Highlights where each stock wins/loses on valuation, health, growth, analyst consensus
- Verdict section recommends one over the other given the data
- Could be expanded to 3-way comparison later
