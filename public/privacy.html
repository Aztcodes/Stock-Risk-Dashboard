Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Aztic is a private beta product. We collect the minimum data necessary to run the service and never sell your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how.

1. Data We Collect

DataWhy we collect itStored where
Email address Authentication and account identification Supabase (auth)
Google profile (name, email, avatar) If you sign in with Google — used for authentication only Supabase (auth)
Report history So you can revisit reports you've generated Supabase (database)
Fetch log timestamps Rate limiting — counting how many reports you've run per hour Supabase (database)
Tickers you search Passed to Anthropic API to generate your report; not stored separately beyond report history Anthropic API (transient)

We do not collect passwords in plaintext, payment information (not applicable in beta), or any data beyond the above.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication: Your email identifies your account and lets you sign in.
  • Report history: Stored so you can revisit previous reports in the History tab.
  • Rate limiting: Fetch log timestamps are counted to enforce per-hour limits and prevent abuse.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Aztic is built on these third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Anthropic — processes your ticker queries via the Claude API to generate reports. Anthropic may log API requests per their Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — hosts authentication and the database that stores your account data and report history. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the Aztic application. Vercel may log request metadata (IP address, browser type). See Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Google — if you use "Continue with Google", Google OAuth handles authentication. See Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

Your report history is retained until you delete it (via the History tab) or request account deletion. Fetch log entries are retained for rolling 30-day windows for rate limiting purposes and then purged.

If you delete your account, all associated data is deleted from our database within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete individual reports — use the delete button in the History tab at any time.
  • Delete all your data — use the "Clear all" option in the History tab.
  • Request account deletion — email us and we will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
  • Request a copy of your data — email us and we will provide a summary of data we hold about you.

6. Security

Your API calls are authenticated with short-lived JWT tokens issued by Supabase. We do not store your password in plaintext — authentication is handled entirely by Supabase. Row-level security policies in our database ensure you can only access your own data.

No security is perfect. We take reasonable steps to protect your data but cannot guarantee against all possible breaches.

7. Children

Aztic is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email us at joshua.ang@hotmail.my. We aim to respond within 14 days.
