HOW THE AZTIC SCORE WORKS
The Aztic Score (0–100) is the sum of three dimensions. Each dimension is split 60% anchored to specific metrics found via live web search, and 40% qualitative judgment for what the metrics miss.
VALUATION · 35 PTS
Anchored 20: P/E vs sector median + P/S vs the company's own 3-year range. Judgment 15: premium/discount justified by quality or growth runway.
FINANCIAL HEALTH · 35 PTS
Anchored 20: debt-to-equity + free cash flow. Judgment 15: margin trend, capital allocation, liquidity.
GROWTH · 30 PTS
Anchored 18: revenue growth YoY + EPS trend over the last 3 quarters. Judgment 12: durability of growth — moat, TAM, product cycle.
Each dimension's note in the breakdown shows the actual values used. Aztic reports are AI-generated and not financial advice.
